**Effective date:** November 5, 2026

RevX connects vehicle owners with mechanics. It is run by Mustaqim Bin Burhanuddin, in
California ("RevX", "we"). Contact: revxtheapp@gmail.com. RevX is for adults 18 and over
in the United States.

## What we collect

- **Account:** email, password (stored by our provider in protected form), display name,
  account type, optional photo, bio and ZIP code.
- **Mechanic details:** business name, service area and radius, specialties, repairs and makes
  offered, year started, where you work, stated prices and pricing text.
- **Vehicles:** make, model, year, color, nickname, type, engine type, mileage and photos.
- **Problems:** descriptions, your answers, urgency, photos and any sound recording you
  add. A recording can capture nearby voices, so please avoid talking during it.
- **Location:** see below.
- **Activity:** swipes, matches, messages, estimates, appointments, jobs, reviews,
  Community posts, replies, votes and groups, blocks and reports.
- **End-of-job answers, if you give them:** what you paid or charged, how the price compared
  with the quote, whether only necessary work was done, whether the repair was explained, and
  the repairs done. Mechanic profiles show totals across several owners, never one job. What a
  mechanic says they did is shown to that job's owner. Amounts are not shown to anyone else.
- **Device:** a push notification token and your notification settings.

We do not use advertising or analytics tools, track you across other companies' apps or
sites, or sell your personal information.

## How we use it

To run the marketplace; show your problems and profile to relevant people; send messages
and notifications; provide AI diagnosis if you agree; prevent abuse and enforce our Terms;
send account email such as password recovery codes; and comply with the law.

## AI diagnosis

AI diagnosis is optional. We ask before your first one, and you can turn it off in Account & Security. If you agree, we send Google's
Gemini service your problem answers, photos and recording. We do not send your name, email
or location. Google's paid service terms say it does not use this content to improve its
products, and it may keep it for up to 55 days to detect abuse. Results can be wrong and do
not replace a hands-on inspection.

**AI training:** we do not currently use your data to train AI models. If that changes,
we will update this policy and ask for your permission first.

## Location

Only after you tap a location control, we collect your approximate device position
(about 100 m), only while the app is open and never in the background. While the app is
open and permission is on, it may refresh up to once every 6 hours. You can type a ZIP code
or address instead.

Other users see only a snapped area (about 1 km) and a rounded distance. **The exception:** a
mechanic you match with receives the precise location of that problem, so they can find the
vehicle. That ends if the match is cancelled, and never includes your profile position.

"Delete my location" in Profile → Edit removes your position and the locations on your posts.

## What other users can see

Your name, photo and bio; for mechanics, your business details and ratings; your problems,
photos and approximate location; your Community activity; and whatever you send in a chat.
Photos may be accessible to anyone who has their web address, so don't include anything
you wouldn't want shared, such as a license plate or home address.

## Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data until you delete it or your account. You can delete your account in
**Profile → Account & Security → Delete account**.

**Deleting your account permanently deletes all data tied to you**, including your profile,
vehicles, problems, photos, recordings, location, messages, reviews, Community posts,
reports and related records. This also removes shared items from other people's accounts,
such as your chats and jobs with them, reviews between you, and replies under your posts.

These limited copies can last a short time after deletion:
- **Backups:** manual backups, kept for up to 30 days.
- **Providers:** records held by providers under their own terms, such as Google's abuse
  logs (up to 55 days) and email delivery logs.
- **Legal requirements:** anything the law requires us to keep, only as long as required.

We can't remove anything someone else already saved outside RevX, such as a screenshot.

## Your rights

Every user can ask to access, correct or delete their data, or get a copy, by emailing
revxtheapp@gmail.com. We won't treat you differently for asking. You can also change your
permissions in your phone's settings.

## Security and children

We use access controls and encrypted connections, but no system is perfectly secure. RevX is
not for anyone under 18. If you think a minor has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes

We'll post changes here and update the date. For important changes, we'll tell you in the
app first. We won't apply a major change to data we already hold without your permission.
