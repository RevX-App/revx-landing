## Direction contract

OWN-WORLD: Smoked black glass ground (#07090B) under one night-drive light. Sky backlight (#38BDF8) for needles, lit readouts and the primary action. Amber (#F5A524) only for warning tell-tales. Red only for "stop driving". Silver wordmark. Barlow / Barlow Condensed (California-plate DIN lineage) with tabular numerals. Bezels as thin rules, dial arcs with tick scales, and LCD message-centre panels. State vocabulary: unlit, lit, warning, dimmed-not-wired.
